The product

Talkbar is an Impetus product: an AI assistant for websites and ecommerce experiences. It helps visitors find answers and shoppers discover products through conversation.

The experience

A single conversational entry point connects people with relevant information across website content, documentation, and product catalogs. Shopping and sales assistant experiences support different visitor needs.

Behind the conversation

Conversation analytics help teams understand what visitors ask, identify content and product gaps, and explore user sentiment and emerging trends.

Getting started

The product setup moves from entering a website URL to adding an embed snippet, then customizing the assistant’s appearance, tone, and responses.
